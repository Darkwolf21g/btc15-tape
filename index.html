Target. Exact when Kalshi can be reached (through a relay). Otherwise it is estimated from the page's own prices over the minute before the open, and the exchanges here sit a few dollars from the official index. Type one target from Robinhood, in either box, and that difference is measured and applied to every window after it. It holds for hours.

      
Sync. Another way to measure the same difference: type Robinhood's big number while it is showing.

      
Why Robinhood looks late. Its big number is the average of the last 60 seconds, the same quantity the contract settles on. When price moves, it trails spot by about 30 seconds. The fast line here is spot. The blue line is that average.

      
Relay. Browsers are not allowed to read Kalshi directly. A small relay of your own (a free Cloudflare Worker; the code is in the README) lets this page read the exact target, the official 60-second average and Kalshi's own Up price. Paste its address here once.

      
Fees. The edge figures take off Robinhood's commission and Kalshi's 1¢ fee, about 2¢ a contract in the middle of the range.

      
The chance shown is a model: a random walk from spot with recently measured volatility. It is an estimate, not advice.
