Target and calibration

Target. Taken from Kalshi when the browser is allowed to reach it. Otherwise the page estimates it from its own prices if it was open in the minute before the window started. Typing Robinhood's target here is always more accurate.

Sync. The exchanges here and the official index (CF Benchmarks BRTI) can differ by a few dollars. Type Robinhood's big number and tap Sync to line the two up. It holds for hours.

Why Robinhood looks late. Its big number is the average of the last 60 seconds, the same quantity the contract settles on. When price moves, it trails spot by about 30 seconds. The fast line here is spot. The blue line is that average.

Fees. The edge figures take off Robinhood's commission and Kalshi's 1¢ fee, about 2¢ a contract in the middle of the range.

The chance shown is a model: a random walk from spot with recently measured volatility. It is an estimate, not advice.
