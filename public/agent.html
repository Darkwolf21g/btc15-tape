How each strategy trades



Distance ignores the model: it buys the side the official index is $15 or more past the target on, spending $100 at the ask (partial fills if the book is thin); holds while the distance stays at least $10; sells at the bid when it shrinks below $10 or the index crosses; after a sell it buys the same side again only once the index is $25 further out than where it sold; a cross restarts on the other side. No trades in the last 60 s of a window; 6 entry attempts per window, 10-second cooldown, $200 realized loss stop. Expect it to pay the spread and fees on every stop-out; the question the session answers is whether the rides to settlement cover them.


Model edge (original rule): buy the side with the largest estimated edge of at least 3¢ after fees. Sell when the bid after fees beats estimated hold value by 3¢. One contract at a time; 1–4 second arrival, original limit, 50% depth, 3 entry attempts per window, 30-second entry cooldown, $3 realized loss stop. The uncalibrated model can be wrong. Zero fills is a valid result.


Stop cancels pending simulations and prevents further entries or exits. Open positions remain pending until Kalshi officially settles them. Sessions are separate from the legacy agent and checkpoint research below. The last 40 sessions are kept on the relay.

The agent runs in the relay when enabled: from the start minute you set (default minute 5) to 15 seconds before each close, every 5 seconds, on Kalshi's own index and book. Its uncalibrated model considers a trade when a side clears 3¢ after fees at the bad end of the model's band and the call has held for two looks: one limit order at the ask, immediate-or-cancel, a quarter Kelly, capped per window. One trade a window, 12 a day, nothing after the daily loss stop, nothing while Kalshi has trading paused.

    
Paper queues a simulated IOC order, waits for the configured delay, then checks fresh depth at or below the original limit. It can fill partially or not at all. Default: 1 second minimum delay, 50% of displayed quantity, conservative whole-cent fees. The separate demo panel tests real API order handling with mock funds; it does not follow production signals. Live execution remains disabled. Controls require the stream token.
