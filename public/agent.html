The agent runs in the relay when enabled: from the start minute you set (default minute 5) to 15 seconds before each close, every 5 seconds, on Kalshi's own index and book. Its uncalibrated model considers a trade when a side clears 3¢ after fees at the bad end of the model's band and the call has held for two looks: one limit order at the ask, immediate-or-cancel, a quarter Kelly, capped per window. One trade a window, 12 a day, nothing after the daily loss stop, nothing while Kalshi has trading paused.

Paper decides and scores without orders. Demo trades Kalshi's demo exchange (its own key pair on the relay). Live trades your account. Controls need the stream token; Live asks you to type LIVE.
